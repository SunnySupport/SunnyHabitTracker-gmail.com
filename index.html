Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Sunny Habit Tracker has no sign-in, and your progress is stored on your device.
  • What are Gamified and Personal mode? Gamified mode has levels, unlocks and premade habits and quests. Personal mode is a blank slate with only what you add yourself. Each mode keeps its own progress, and you can switch in Settings.
  • How do I reset my progress? Open Settings and scroll to the Danger Zone. You can reset one mode’s progress or delete all save data for both modes.
  • How do I stop hydration reminders? Turn off notifications for Sunny in your iPhone’s Settings app, under Notifications.
  • What happens to my data if I delete the app? Because it’s stored only on your device, deleting the app deletes your progress.
