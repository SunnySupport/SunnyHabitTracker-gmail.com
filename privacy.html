☀️ Sunny Habit Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Sunny Habit Tracker (“the app”) is a habit-tracking app. This page explains what information the app handles and how.

Information we store

The app stores the following directly on your device, using standard local app storage:

  • Your chosen hero/player name
  • Habits, quests, and achievements you create or complete
  • Your streak, level, and progress data
  • App preferences (sound, dark mode, tips, music track, game mode)

None of this information is transmitted to us or to any third party. It never leaves your device unless you uninstall the app or clear its data, at which point it is deleted.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, the app may send local reminders (for example, hydration reminders). These are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device and are not based on any data sent to a server.

Third-party services

The app does not currently use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. All data described above is stored locally on the device and is not collected by us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same location with a new “last updated” date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to sunnyhabittracker@gmail.com.
